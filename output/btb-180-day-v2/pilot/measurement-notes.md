## Future measurements, only when supplied

All actual publication dates, snapshots, reach, shares, saves, attributed follows and calculated rates remain null. Missing information is unknown, not zero. No account was accessed and no performance was collected.

After any separately authorised publication, use a consistent seven-day post snapshot. Record actual publication and snapshot dates so posts of different ages are not compared as peers. The editorial slot is not evidence of publication. Keep native shares, saves and attributable follows distinct; do not rename shares as DM sends.

Use shares / accounts reached × 1,000 and saves / accounts reached × 1,000 only when reach is observed and positive. Use native post-attributed follows / accounts reached × 1,000 only where that attribution exists. Daily net follower change is a different account-level metric and cannot replace attributed follows. Do not use views as unique reach or sum post reach as unique audience.

Compare individual values and group medians descriptively after enough comparable observations exist. Flag unequal post age, unusual exposure and differences in topic, CTA, hook length and audience. A four-post group has substantial uncertainty; an observed difference does not show that the surface caused performance. With no effective C posts, do not calculate or describe a C result. Do not declare a winning treatment or rewrite the library from imagined results.
